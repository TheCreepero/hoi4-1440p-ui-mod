# Engine Facts

Everything the mod relies on about how the Clausewitz engine treats `.gui` files, one row per fact, with how sure we are. [Design Rules](Design-Rules.md) explains the rules that follow from these facts; this page is the register an agent scans before proposing a layout or asking for a launch.

Status values:

- **confirmed**: seen in game, with the phase and launch that showed it.
- **provisional**: used in a shipped screen but judged only once, or judged from a screenshot rather than a dedicated check.
- **untested**: assumed from the files or from a tool, not yet seen in game.
- **disproved**: tried and found wrong; kept so nobody tries it again.

Add a row when a launch or an experiment teaches something, and move a row up when a check confirms it. `tools/render_gui.py` draws what the **confirmed** and **provisional** rows describe; the "Renderer" column says whether it does.

## Scripted GUIs

A scripted GUI is a container of the mod's own that script fills and listens to (`common/scripted_guis/`, described in the game's `_documentation.md` there). It changes the checksum. Rows X1 to X5 come from the probe of D0, X6 to X25 from the probe that opened D2 ([notes](phases/D2-army-readiness.md)), X26 to X31 from building the pages of [Scripted GUIs](Scripted-GUIs.md), X32 to X49 from the probe for the role-play pages ([E0](phases/E0-roleplay-probe.md)), X50 to X56 from building and polishing them, X57 from the rework of the manager pages and X66 to X70 from the officers another mod adds (the same day, [E2 notes](phases/E2-officer-records.md)), X58 to X65 from the portraits, flags and victory points of 2026-10-13, read in the game's documentation and in The New Order and then seen in the checks of that day ([E1 notes](phases/E1-war-diary.md), "Game checks"); all three probes were removed again.
