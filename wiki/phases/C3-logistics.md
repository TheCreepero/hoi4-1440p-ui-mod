# Phase C3: Logistics tab and Stockpile window

The orchestrator writes Scope, Roles and handoffs, Decisions and Close; the designer's work lands in Inventory and Proposal; the builder writes Build and the Fix rounds; the game checker adds the rows of Game checks. The [Roadmap](../Roadmap.md) keeps only the status and a link here.

## Scope

- Asked by the user on 2026-10-18 (with `/phase` and a screenshot of their own game, Italy 1941 in their playset): "Redesign this Logistics tab and the stockpile view. The tab is narrow, so all the information in there is very small. The graph in the stockpile view is too small and not very useful in its current form. If there's any more information that could be displayed here at a glance (such as, how long a shortage is estimated to last), it would be good."
- Vanilla file and windows: `countrylogisticsview.gui`: the tab `countrylogisticsview` (550 wide, full height, lines 3-622), the Stockpile window `logistics_info_window` (395 wide, 70% high: the chart `linechart`, a `LineChartType` `GFX_logistics_details_linechart` of 224x150 with a 1 px line, Days / Months / Years, and the list of variants), the fuel window `logistics_fuel_window` (395 wide), and their rows: the three equipment rows of land, naval and air (565x57), the three "other" rows, the resource items, the fuel line and the variant row `logistics_info_equipment_entry` (351x40).
- What the vanilla screen crams or hides (from the screenshot): every value of an equipment row in boxes of about 40 px with a small font and no caption but the header strip; the resource icons in a column of their own with no numbers; the chart 224x150 with a hairline and three labels (1K, 0, -1K) and no saying what it plots; the variant list a column of identical names told apart only by a tiny flag.
- Possible addition (the user's "how long a shortage will last"): a value the vanilla window cannot show (no new live values in a vanilla window), so only a scripted GUI could add it. The design says whether script can compute it and how, and the user decides.
- Out of scope: the fuel map mode, the supply map mode, the Army Readiness page (D2), which already has a table of land equipment (stock, in the field, needed) from script.
